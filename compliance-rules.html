Compliance Rules

Client
The LEGO Group
Role
Visual design
Scope
Icons, cards, badges
Year
To be confirmed

The brief

Compliance rules are part of every LEGO employee's working life, but they usually arrive as long, dreary pages of text that are hard to digest and remember. The task was a visual identity that simplifies the key principles for new and long-standing employees.

The idea

A series of colourful illustrated icons to replace the image of the dusty old guidebook and make the principles memorable, fun and easy to use.

The set

The icons were printed as cards and badges and given to employees for daily use.
