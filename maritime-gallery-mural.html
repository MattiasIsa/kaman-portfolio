The Singapore Maritime Gallery Mural

Client
Singapore Maritime Gallery
Role
Designer, at The Press Room
Scope
Mural, infographics, illustration
Year
To be confirmed

The brief

Show the diversity and vibrancy of Singapore's maritime industry to the general public, and tell the story of its maritime history in a way that is fun, interactive and educational.

The idea

A main mural to welcome visitors at the gallery entrance. The same design was printed on limited-edition MRT train cards to mark the gallery's opening.

Infographics

Infographics for the gallery walls built public awareness of a diverse, dynamic industry in a modern, light-hearted style.
