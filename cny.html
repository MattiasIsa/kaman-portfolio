Brand campaign · The LEGO Group

Let Your CNY Imagination Run Wild

Client
The LEGO Group, China
Role
Senior Art Director
Scope
TV commercials, key visuals, retail, out-of-home
Year
To be confirmed

The brief

Promote LEGO's Chinese New Year sets with a TV commercial and print ads that mean something to families in China at the most important time of their year.

The idea

Bring children's wild imagination to life inside two moments nearly every Chinese family knows: the long train ride home, and the reunion dinner.

Film 1The journey home Video file to be added

The journey home

Families often spend long hours on the train, from the city where they work to the hometown where the grandparents live. Children get restless on the ride. In the film, a child's imagination takes over the carriage as they play with the LEGO Lion Dance set.

Film 2The reunion Video file to be added

The reunion

While the older generation gets the reunion dinner ready, the children and teenagers gather to play. For many it's one of the few times a year that cousins from different households see each other, and the moment a LEGO set can bring them together.

Key visuals

Two key visuals carried the same idea into print, one for each set. Both started as pencil sketches.

The Lion Dance

Not the lion dance you'd expect. It's a DJ party, where the lions dance on the mixer and the loudspeakers are Chinese gongs.

The Temple Fair

An unexpected view of the New Year marketplace: a celebration in the clouds, a temple fair in the sky.

In the world

The key visuals then ran across digital promotions, store-front windows and outdoor 3D billboards.
