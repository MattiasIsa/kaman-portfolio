E-commerce campaign · The LEGO Group

Indulge in Your Passions

Client
The LEGO Group, Tmall flagship store
Role
Senior Art Director
Scope
Key visuals, product visuals
Year
2024

The brief

The 2024 Double 11 campaign for the LEGO flagship store on Tmall, aimed at adults who want to make time for LEGO building despite the grind of everyday life.

The idea

Three key visuals, each showing a different adult and the small moment of enjoyment they find in a busy day.

The details

A young professional working overtime, a university student busy with school, and a young man balancing work with his other interests.

Familiar objects such as coffee cups, stationery, headphones, instant noodles and exercise equipment bring each character's daily life into the picture. In the first visual, a young working woman is surrounded by the signs of a long day at her desk: coffee, stationery, an alarm set for 8am and notes reminding her to work overtime. At the centre is the LEGO Art Mona Lisa, the thing she builds for herself.

The everyday objects are treated in a single colour so the LEGO sets stand out at the centre. The contrast shows how building can bring colour and a little wonder into a repetitive day.

Product visuals

A set of product visuals in the same art direction promoted other sets aimed at adults, each flanked by the same everyday objects.
