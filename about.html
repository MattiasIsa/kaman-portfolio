About Kaman

I'm Kaman Wong, a senior art director and visual designer.

I spent ten years at The LEGO Group, first in Billund making global digital campaigns, then in Shanghai as Senior Art Director, leading art directors, designers, copywriters and production partners.

Before that I freelanced for Ogilvy & Mather in Japan and for clients in Singapore, and started out as a graphic designer at The Press Room. I hold an MFA in graphic design and advertising from Tama Art University in Tokyo, where I studied on a Japanese government scholarship.

I work across campaigns, film, books, packaging and illustration. I've lived and worked in Singapore, Japan, Denmark and China, and I'm now back in Singapore.

Freelance, available for projects and open to the right full-time role. Singapore-based, working worldwide.
