Product campaign · The LEGO Group

LEGO DOTS Launch

Client
The LEGO Group, China
Role
Senior Art Director
Scope
Metro installation, retail, packaging, social
Year
2020

The brief

LEGO DOTS launched in 2020: colourful 2D tiles made for children's creative self-expression, unlike the bricks used for houses and vehicles. The task was to launch it in Shanghai with a consistent, eye-catching experience that made children want to explore it.

The idea

A striking installation that would drive publicity and social buzz in the busy landscape of Shanghai.

The design

For the metro installation I designed pillars using the most recognisable illustrations from the products, including emojis and a unicorn, to catch the eye of children walking by. On the other sides, the bracelets were recreated at an exaggerated scale to show their detail.

My designs went to a professional LEGO builder, who built them out of LEGO bricks and installed the final pieces in the metro station.

A QR code on two sides of each pillar led to the campaign mini program, where people could learn about the products and find the nearest store.

In store

The campaign extended into retail, where I adapted the global design across the window frontage and display cases for one consistent look throughout the store.

Children who bought LEGO DOTS during the campaign received a specially designed bag, and an engagement table in the same art direction invited them to try the tiles for themselves.

Social

The same visual direction carried into social media advertising, keeping the campaign recognisable at every touchpoint.
