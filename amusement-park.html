TV commercial · The LEGO Group

Have a Blast at the Amusement Park

Client
The LEGO Group, LEGO Friends
Role
Art Director
Scope
TV commercial, YouTube social video
Year
To be confirmed

The brief

LEGO Friends was struggling to win over new girls. Many of them didn't find the products fun or trendy.

The idea

Make a TV commercial and a YouTube video in the style of the content girls already love: influencers' videos and quirky TikTok shorts.

Film 1TV commercial Video file to be added

TV commercial

We cut back and forth between real scenes of kids having a blast at an amusement park and scenes of them role-playing with the set at home. We picked out key product features and shot matching live-action scenes, so one could match-cut into the other.

Film 2YouTube social video Video file to be added

YouTube social video

This one took a YouTuber approach: two lively girls host the show and take on mini challenges using the set.

The styling of the sets for the commercial and the YouTube video.
