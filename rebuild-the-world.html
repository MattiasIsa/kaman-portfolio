The brief

As part of LEGO's Rebuild the World campaign in China, encourage children to reimagine the world with LEGO bricks. The obstacle: parents of six-to-eight-year-olds saw LEGO as no different from other toys, a matter of following instructions.

The idea

Find real children in China who had solved everyday problems by rebuilding with LEGO bricks, and tell their stories in a semi-documentary style.

The structure

Each film follows three beats to build an emotional connection with the child: show the problem, show the work in progress, show the child's solution.
